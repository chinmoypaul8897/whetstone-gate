6. THE SPLIT'S BIAS DIRECTION - TESTED, AND IT IS WIDER THAN STATED
=====================================================================
C6's claim - "a PARAPHRASE counts as IMPROVISED, so the corpus fraction is a LOWER
BOUND and improvisation an UPPER BOUND" - IS CORRECT. All ten constructed cases land
as its model predicts:
  verbatim in a sentence -> CORPUS      ONE WORD CHANGED    -> IMPROVISED
  whitespace re-flowed   -> CORPUS      RE-CASED            -> IMPROVISED
  NFD-decomposed         -> CORPUS      PARTIAL QUOTE (80%) -> IMPROVISED
  NBSP for a space       -> CORPUS      SPLICED mid-entry   -> IMPROVISED
                                        paraphrase          -> IMPROVISED

BUT "PARAPHRASE" IS NOT THE WHOLE BIAS (OF-49):
  - CASE-ONLY variation lands IMPROVISED. _normalise is NFC + whitespace collapse with
    NO CASE FOLDING, and an LLM re-casing borrowed text is ordinary, not paraphrase.
  - VERBATIM REUSE OF A DIFFERENT OFFERED ENTRY LANDS IMPROVISED. classify_provenance
    is handed ONE seed - this turn's - so exact, unaltered reuse of the entry offered
    five turns ago is recorded as improvisation. THAT IS NOT A PARAPHRASE; IT IS
    CORPUS TEXT COUNTED AS ORIGINAL.

IS "IMPROVISATION" THE NUMBER A READER WILL TAKE IT FOR? NO. A reader meeting "X% of
successful attacks were the attacker's own improvisation" understands "the model
invented this." The field measures "this turn's output did not contain THIS TURN's
offered entry as an exact substring after NFC and whitespace collapse." The gap is
wide, one-directional, and - with F-2 - large. THE BIAS DIRECTION IS STATED CORRECTLY
AND THE BIAS MAGNITUDE IS UNDERSTATED. C18 must publish the operational definition
beside the number, not only the direction.
