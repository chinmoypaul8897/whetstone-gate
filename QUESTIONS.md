# QUESTIONS.md — every ambiguity hit, and every ruling made

**A ruling that exists only in a chat does not exist.** Rulings are recorded here **verbatim**,
before anything else is touched (`CLAUDE.md` hard rule 5), and a recorded ruling binds every later
session.

---

## The format

Every item is a numbered question. **Nothing is deleted; a question is closed by filling in its
ruling, not by removing it.**

```
### Q-NNN — <one-line title>
**Raised by:** <chunk> <BUILD|REVIEW|FIX> · **Date:** <ISO-8601> · **Status:** OPEN | RULED | WITHDRAWN
**Blocking:** <what cannot proceed until this is answered — or "nothing; unblocked work continued">
**Deviation class:** A (changes meaning, behaviour, or a reported number — STOP and ask) |
                     B (implementation choice within spec — do it, record it, judged at review) |
                     C (cosmetic — one line) | n/a
**Context:** <the exact text of the spec / process / prompt clauses that conflict or are silent,
quoted, with file and section>
**Options seen:**
  1. <option> — <consequence>
  2. <option> — <consequence>
**Default taken pending a ruling:** <what the session actually did, and why it is the reversible
choice> — or "none; work stopped on this item"

**RULING (architect, <ISO-8601>):** *<verbatim. Not paraphrased.>*
```

**Deviation classes are `CLAUDE.md` hard rule 2.** A **Class A** item may not be defaulted past — the
session stops that item, records it, and continues any unblocked work (hard rule 1). A session that
stops on a real ambiguity has succeeded.
